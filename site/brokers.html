Brokers

The single most important thing to understand before you sign up: whose name are your shares actually held in?

CDP-linked: shares sit in a government-run depository under your own name — you can move them between brokers freely. Custodian: the broker (or its nominee) holds shares on your behalf, which is often cheaper and easier for overseas markets, but moving out can be slower.

## What was built
A static, visual-heavy site explaining Singapore investing to laymen:
- 18 products, 12 methods and 7 broker types.
- Pros and cons, a growth↔dividend meter, and step-by-step apply/register guides.
- A fee-drag calculator with like-for-like cost groups, plus a compare page and a quiz.

Every fee figure comes from a modular scraper with 34 adapters, each figure stamped with its date and source.
